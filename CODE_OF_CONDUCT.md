# Code of Conduct

OpenMaintainer is for maintainers and contributors who want calmer, clearer open-source work.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and direct.
- Assume good intent while asking for evidence.
- Keep technical disagreement focused on the work.
- Make space for contributors using English as a second language.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks
- Publishing private information
- Discriminatory language or conduct
- Repeated disruption after maintainer requests to stop

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, block participants, or restrict access when conduct harms the project.
